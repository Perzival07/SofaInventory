# 🛋️ The Sofa Studio & Manufacturing Co. — Staff Operational Manual
### বারাসাত শোরুম ও কারখানা সহায়িকা (Barasat Showroom & Factory Guide)

This internal system is designed for our counter staff, showroom managers, factory supervisors, and karigars. **No login or password is required** — simply open the system on any counter PC, tablet, or mobile device.

The interface supports both **English** and **বাংলা (Bengali)** via the language button in the header.

---

## 🧭 Main Sections Overview

Use the top navigation tabs to switch between the 4 business areas:

1. **Retail & Khata (বিক্রি ও খাতা)**: Counter billing, cash memos, tax invoices, Barasat home delivery, and customer credit ledger.
2. **Production WIP (কারখানা ও উৎপাদন)**: In-house furniture manufacturing across 9 stages, karigar piece-rate wages, and quality checks.
3. **Job Work Vendors (জব ওয়ার্ক ও কারিগর)**: Material issued to polishing, carving, or stitching vendors, delivery challans, and returns.
4. **Raw Materials (কাঁচামাল ভাণ্ডার)**: Timber, ply, foam, fabric dye lots, scrap offcuts, and seasoning checks.

## 💰 1. Billing & Retail Sales (কাউন্টার বিক্রয়)

### Creating a Sale:
1. On the **Retail & Khata** tab, tap **"+ Create New Sale"**.
2. Enter the customer's **Name** and **Mobile Number**.
3. Select the **Delivery Zone**:
   - *Barasat Town / Champadali*: ₹500
   - *Madhyamgram / Hridaypur*: ₹800
   - *Habra / Ashoknagar*: ₹1,200
   - *North Kolkata (Dum Dum, Salt Lake, New Town)*: ₹1,800
4. If delivering to an upper floor without a service lift, select the **Floor Number** (₹150 per floor from 2nd floor up).
5. Choose products and quantities.
6. Select **Payment Mode**:
   - **Cash**: Customer pays cash at counter.
   - **UPI**: Customer scans Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm QR code.
   - **Khata Credit (বাকি / ধার)**: Adds the sale balance to customer's ledger account.
   - **Split Payment**: Partial advance with remainder on delivery.
7. Click **"Generate Document & Save Sale"**.

### Cash Memo vs Tax Invoice:
- **Cash Memo (CM-...)**: Generated automatically while the business is below the ₹40 Lakh limit (GST unregistered). Shows items, delivery, and total with zero tax clutter.
- **Tax Invoice (INV-...)**: Generated when the GST Regime is active. Automatically splits CGST (9%) and SGST (9%) for West Bengal buyers, or full IGST (18%) for out-of-state buyers.
- **Printing**: Click **"Print Cash Memo"** or **"Print Tax Invoice"** for a clean, receipt-sized printout with bilingual details.

## 📒 2. Managing Khata / Udhaar Accounts (গ্রাহকের খাতা)

When trusted local customers take furniture on installment or partial credit:
1. In the **Khata (Customer Credit Ledger)** table, find the customer.
2. View their **Total Purchases**, **Total Paid**, and **Current Outstanding Balance**.
3. When the customer pays an installment:
   - Click **"Record Payment"**.
   - Enter the payment amount (Cash or UPI).
   - Add notes if needed (e.g. *"Installment #2 via PhonePe"*).
   - Click **"Confirm Payment"**.
4. The customer's balance updates instantly. When the balance reaches ₹0, the account is marked **"SETTLED"**.

## 🔨 3. In-House Factory WIP & Karigars (কারখানা ও কারিগর)

Track the 9 sequential stages of sofa and furniture production:
1. **Stage 1 (Carpentry Frame)**: Timber cutting & joinery. Check wood moisture!
2. **Stage 2 (Webbing & Springing)**: Elastic webbing & zig-zag springs.
3. **Stage 3 (Foam Profiling)**: Bonding 28D/32D/40D foam to wooden frame.
4. **Stage 4 (Cushion Core)**: High-resilience core wrapping.
5. **Stage 5 (Pattern Cutting)**: Fabric/leatherette cutting. Check dye lot!
6. **Stage 6 (Sewing)**: Piping, zipper insertion & stitching.
7. **Stage 7 (Upholstery & Tufting)**: Stretching, staple fastening & deep tufting.
8. **Stage 8 (Wood Polishing)**: Sanding, sealing, PU coat or melamine buffing.
9. **Stage 9 (QC & Packaging)**: Final inspection, multi-box carton verification.

### Logging a Production Entry:
1. Under **Production WIP**, tap **"+ Log Stage Entry"**.
2. Select the **Work Order** and the **Production Stage**.
3. Select the **Karigar** who performed the work.
4. Enter the **Units Completed** and **Piece-Rate Wage (₹)** earned.
5. Toggle the **Quality Inspection Passed** switch if approved.
6. Click **"Save Entry"**.

## 🚚 4. Job Work & Outsourced Karigars (জব ওয়ার্ক)

When sending timber or frames out for specialized polishing, CNC carving, or upholstery:

> **IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTE**: Raw material sent to outside karigars is **OUR legal inventory asset**. It does not leave our balance sheet.

1. **Issuing Material**:
   - Go to **Job Work Vendors** and tap **"Issue Material to Vendor"**.
   - Select the vendor and enter material and quantity.
   - An **Internal Delivery Challan (IDC-...)** or statutory **Rule 45 Challan** is generated.
2. **Receiving Returned Goods**:
   - Click **"Reconcile Return"** next to the active order.
   - Enter finished units received and scrap/waste returned.
   - Check against expected scrap allowance (e.g. 5% sawdust/shavings).
   - Enter vendor's service charges for 3-way matching.

## 🌲 5. Raw Material Store & Monsoon Checks (কাঁচামাল ও বর্ষার সতর্কতা)

### Timber Moisture Check:
- During North 24 Parganas monsoon months (June to September), humidity exceeds 85%.
- Check the timber moisture gauge on the Raw Material card.
- **Any timber lot with moisture >14% is automatically quarantined** and cannot be issued to Carpentry Frame until kiln/air dried.

### Fabric Dye Lots:
- Every fabric roll has a **Dye Lot Number** (e.g. `LOT-BLU-402`).
- Never mix different dye lots on the same sofa or dining chair set to avoid visible color shade differences.

### Offcut Scrap Remnants:
- Plywood strips and timber cut-offs under standard sizes are logged in the **Scrap Remnants** section so karigars can reuse them for corner blocks and internal bracing before cutting fresh sheets.

## 🛡️ 6. Turnover Watchdog & GST Switchover (টার্নওভার সতর্কবার্তা)

At the top of the screen:
- **Green (Safe)**: Under ₹30 Lakhs. Continue operating as unregistered.
- **Yellow (Prepare)**: Between ₹30 and ₹35 Lakhs. Begin assembling paperwork for GST registration.
- **Orange (Critical)**: Between ₹35 and ₹38 Lakhs. Festive season surge will cross threshold. Apply for GSTIN now.
- **Red (Blocked)**: At ₹38 Lakhs. The system alerts that GST registration is legally mandatory.

### When GSTIN Arrives:
1. Click the **"Unregistered"** shield badge in the header.
2. Enter our 15-digit GSTIN (e.g. `19AAAAA0000A1Z5`) and registration effective date.
3. Click **"Switch to GST Registered Regime"**.
4. The system automatically switches to issuing Tax Invoices with CGST/SGST splits.
5. You can view and download the **Section 18(1)(a) Transitional Credit Report** to claim back GST paid on all stock purchased in the past 12 months!
