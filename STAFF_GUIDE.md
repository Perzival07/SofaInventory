# 🛋️ The Sofa Studio — Shop Staff Quick Guide

Welcome to the Furniture Inventory System! This tool is open to all shop staff on any phone, tablet, or counter computer. There is **no username or password** needed — just open the shop link in your browser.

---

### 1. Daily Dashboard Overview

When you open the screen, you will see:
- **Top Numbers**: Total sofas/furniture units in stock and total warehouse value in Indian Rupees (**₹**).
- **Search Bar**: Quickly find any sofa or furniture by typing its name, color, or type (e.g., *"Velvet"*, *"Recliner"*, *"Navy"*).
- **Category Buttons**: Tap **Sofa**, **Recliner**, **Dining Set**, **Bed**, etc., to filter immediately.
- **Stock Badges**:
  - 🟢 **In Stock** (4+ units available)
  - 🟡 **Low Stock** (1 to 3 units left — order soon!)
  - 🔴 **Out of Stock** (0 units available)

---

### 2. How to Add a Brand-New Furniture Item
*(Use this when introducing a new model or design to the showroom)*

1. Tap the golden **"+ Add New Furniture"** button in the top right.
2. Enter the **Name** (e.g., *"3-Seater Chesterfield Sofa - Grey Velvet"*).
3. Select the **Category** from the drop-down.
4. Enter the **Initial Quantity** arriving at the shop.
5. Enter the **Unit Cost (₹)** paid to the supplier for each piece.
6. *(Optional)* Add a note (e.g., *"Supplier: Heritage Furnishings, Inv #501"*).
7. Tap **"Add Furniture to Stock"**. The item will appear immediately with its initial batch logged.

---

### 3. How to Restock / Renew Stock (Core Daily Action)
*(Use this whenever a new truck or batch of furniture arrives at the shop)*

1. Find the item in the list and tap the golden **"Restock"** button.
2. Enter **Units Being Added** (e.g., `+5`).
3. Enter the **New Batch Cost per Unit (₹)**:
   - *Note: Supplier prices change over time! If this new batch was bought at a different rate, enter the new price here. The main dashboard will automatically update to reflect this latest batch cost.*
4. The **Date** defaults to today, but you can change it if logging an earlier arrival.
5. *(Recommended)* Enter the **Supplier Name or Invoice Number** in the note field.
6. Tap **"Confirm & Update Stock"**.
   - The stock count goes up immediately.
   - The new cost becomes the active unit cost.
   - A permanent record is saved to the item's history.

---

### 4. How to View Restock History
*(Use this to check past supplier prices, invoice numbers, or when stock arrived)*

1. Tap the blue **"History"** button next to any item.
2. A timeline will appear showing every batch ever received:
   - Date of delivery
   - How many units arrived (`+N units`)
   - What was paid per unit at that time
   - Total batch cost
   - Supplier / invoice note
3. Tap **"Close History"** when done.

---

### 5. Using on Mobile or Counter Tablets
- **On Phones & Tablets**: Tap any button easily (designed with large finger-friendly buttons).
- **Table vs Cards**: On tablets and desktops, you can toggle between **Table View** and **Card Grid View** using the layout buttons next to the sort box.
